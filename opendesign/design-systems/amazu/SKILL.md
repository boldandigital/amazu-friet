# Amazú Friet — Brand System

## Taste dials (LOCKED)

Elegant, warm, artisanal. Not fast-food. Gold on deep plum, never gold on white.
Belgian technique, Portuguese soul. The house is small, the potatoes are sweet,
the roots go back into the forest. Confident, never loud.

## Brand foundations

- **Name:** Amazú Friet (the seal reads *Amazú Doces*, the product photography
  reads *Amazú Pulp*. All three belong to the same house.)
- **Positioning:** Belgian double-fry technique applied to Brazilian sweet
  potato, built on Amazonian ingredients and made in Belém.
- **Signature lines:** `Premium Batata`, `Raízes da Floresta`,
  `Belgian technique, Portuguese soul`, `Friet não é acompanhamento. É prato.`
- **Product pillars:** three fries, three freshly pressed juices, two sweets.
- **Raízes da Floresta** is brand storytelling, not a literal claim about where
  the potatoes grow. Never state it as a sourcing fact.

## Canvas and colour

One permanent appearance. There is no light mode and no theme switch.

| Token | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `--plum` | `#411a3b` | Amazú Plum. The canvas. Every major section sits on it. |
| `--plum-raised` | `#4d2145` | Raised Plum. Functional grouping only, never a card grid. |
| `--gold-light` | `#efc985` | Light Gold. Headings and body copy. |
| `--gold` | `#e2b872` | Amazú Gold. Primary actions, compact controls. |
| `--gold-muted` | `#c2a67d` | Muted Gold. Captions, secondary text. |
| `--gold-line` | `#9a7a4a` | Gold Line. Hairlines only. |

- Never replace the plum canvas with white, cream or any light surface.
- Gold fills are reserved for compact controls. Large gold fields are not a
  surface.
- No metallic gradients, no glow, no glitter on UI text. Flat gold only.

## Typography

- **Display:** Bodoni Moda 400 / 500 / 600. A Didone serif, matching the
  Amazú seal. Use for headings and for the wordmark.
- **Body:** Jost 400 / 500 / 600. A geometric sans. Use for everything else,
  including prices, labels and navigation.
- Wordmark treatment: plum fill with a gold hairline stroke, wide tracking.
- No text inside busy image areas. Keep type on the plum canvas or on a clean
  plate.

## Layout

- Radius 2px. Border 1px. 4px baseline grid.
- Max content width 1200px.
- Section spacing 96–128px on desktop, 56–72px on mobile.
- One primary CTA per view.
- Left-align practical information (prices, sizes, ingredients). Centred
  compositions are allowed when they echo the seal.

## Imagery

Warm, tactile food photography. Deep backgrounds, plum or dark neutral
settings. Natural light, real texture, visible steam. Never a purple filter on
food. Show the kraft box and the bamboo fork; they are part of the product.

## Hard rules

1. Plum canvas, gold foreground. One appearance, always.
2. No em-dashes in body copy.
3. No exclamation marks in body copy.
4. No fast-food slang, no exaggerated superlatives, no elitist language.
5. No invented health, sustainability or ingredient-origin claims.
6. Do not wrap every section in a panel. Avoid repetitive card grids.
7. Gold is flat. No metallic gradients, no glow, no glitter.
8. Keep text out of busy image areas.
9. Prices are in R$ and are exact: R$ 29,90 (Friet Doce), R$ 32,90 (Friet
   Tucupi Preto), R$ 12,00 (Pulp). Queijo Marajó has no published price.
10. One primary CTA per view.
