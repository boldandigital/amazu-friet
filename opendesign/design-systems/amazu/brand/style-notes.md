# Style notes

## Canvas

One permanent appearance. The plum canvas `#411a3b` is the house. There is no
light mode, no theme switch, no pale footer and no white card. Every major
section sits on the same plum, and the sections are separated by space,
hairlines and type scale, not by panels.

`#4d2145` (Raised Plum) exists for functional grouping only: a price block, a
list, a caption that needs a little more presence than the canvas gives. It
is not a card background and it does not become a grid of boxes.

## Gold

`#e2b872` is the action colour. Fill it only on compact controls: a button, a
pill, a small tag. `#efc985` (Light Gold) carries headings and body copy.
`#c2a67d` (Muted Gold) carries captions and secondary text. `#9a7a4a` (Gold
Line) is a hairline.

Gold is always flat. No metallic gradient, no glow, no shadow shimmer, no
glitter, no gradient text. The gold in this identity comes from foil on a
seal, and foil reads as a single warm colour, not as a light show.

## Type

Bodoni Moda for display, Jost for everything else. The wordmark is Bodoni at
wide tracking with a gold hairline stroke over a plum fill.

Jost runs at 300 weight for the large hero statement in the original Figma
mock; on a plum canvas that drops to unreadable, so the hero statement runs at
400 and the gold gets the emphasis instead.

Prices are set in Jost, tabular where possible, with the R$ prefix in muted
gold and the value in light gold. Never drop the comma decimal.

## Composition

Practical information is left-aligned: prices, sizes, ingredients, opening
information. Centred compositions are permitted where they echo the seal, in
the wordmark and the closing statement.

Avoid repetitive card grids and avoid wrapping every section in a panel.
Rhythm comes from generous spacing: 96–128px between sections on desktop,
56–72px on mobile, on a 4px baseline grid.

## Imagery

Warm, tactile food photography on deep backgrounds, plum or dark neutral. Real
texture, steam, the sheen of the glaze, the crumb of the farofa. Light falls
naturally. The kraft box and the bamboo fork stay in frame because they are
part of the product.

Never a purple filter over food. Never a flat-lay with no shadow. Never a
stock hand reaching in. Never text over a busy part of the frame.

## Motion

Restrained. The house is unhurried. Elements may arrive with a short fade and
a small lift. Nothing bounces, nothing spins, nothing parallaxes, and nothing
depends on a scroll to become legible. If a reader never scrolls, the page
must still read.

## Imagery and type

Keep type off busy image areas. The seal sits on the plum canvas at the top
of the frame. The wordmark sits across the hero with the product shot behind
it, never on top of the busiest part of the food.
