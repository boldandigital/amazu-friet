# Voice and tone

## The house in one line

Refined, welcoming, sensory, confident, artisanal, warm, considered.

Amazú Friet is a small kitchen in Belém that fries sweet potato the Belgian
way and dresses it with what the Amazon gives. It talks like the person who
runs it: precise about the craft, generous about the food, never loud.

## How the copy sounds

- **Sensory before descriptive.** "Double-fried", "crisp", "tucupi black
  fermented eight hours" beat "delicious" and "amazing".
- **Confident, not boastful.** State what the food is. Let it speak.
- **Warm in the second person, not in the adjectives.** "Everything is chosen
  at the market" beats "we are passionate about quality".
- **Short sentences at the point of appetite.** Long sentences belong in the
  story, not the menu.
- **Portuguese stays Portuguese.** Product names, place names and the house
  line stay in pt-BR. "Friet não é acompanhamento. É prato." is not
  translated.

## What the copy never does

- Never use em-dashes in body copy.
- Never use exclamation marks in body copy.
- No fast-food slang, no hype, no "crazy", "insane", "next level".
- No exaggerated superlatives. No "the best in Brazil".
- No elitist or exclusionary language. The house is warm to everyone.
- No invented health claims ("healthy", "guilt-free", "low calorie").
- No invented sustainability claims ("sustainable", "eco", "carbon neutral").
- No unsupported ingredient-origin claims. "Raízes da Floresta" is the name
  the house is built on, not a statement about where the potatoes grow.
- No fabricated social proof, scarcity, urgency or crossed-out reference
  prices.
- No fake testimonials, ratings or press logos.

## Words the house uses

Belgian technique, Portuguese soul. Premium batata. Raízes da Floresta.
Friet. Kraft box. Bamboo fork. Ver-o-Peso. Double-fry.

## Words the house avoids

Artisanal (it is stated once, then shown), authentic, delicious, yummy,
crazy, crazy good, must try, limited edition, handcrafted (use "made by hand
at the market" instead), premium (it is already in the name), fire,
insane, next level, guilt-free.
